FROZEN MODEL CONTRACT

How browser-only works

01

Local preprocessing

168-h maximum history, 1-h grid, frozen robust normalization and exact 1024-event selection.

02

Anchor SAHG

Frozen pretrained SAHG backbone produces the 256-dimensional donor/OOD anchor representation.

03

5-model DR ensemble

Five frozen targeted models produce action-specific risk, g-computation risk and propensities.

04

Donor support

Anonymous donor group IDs preserve unique-patient KNN, adaptive kernel, ESS and distance calculations.

05

Safety gate

Propensity, donor support, regimen-node support, directional agreement, uncertainty and OOD are evaluated.

06

coverage_p050

Frozen penalized risk-difference selection plus A4 confirmation, A5 rescue and true abstention.

DEPLOYMENT MODEL

No model server after publication

The final package is a static GitHub Pages site. ONNX inference, donor search, OOD and frozen policy logic execute locally in the visitor's browser.

Before public release, independently confirm that distribution of the donor-derived runtime is permitted under the applicable MIMIC-IV/PhysioNet data-use terms and your institution's governance requirements.
